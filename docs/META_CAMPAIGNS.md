# Meta Ads Campaign Menu — RaikanBersama

Product: digital wedding invitation SaaS, Malaysia, Bahasa Malaysia audience.
Plans: Lite RM29 / Pro RM49 / Elite RM69. Draft is free, pay to publish.
Measurable events today: `PageView`, `AddToCart` (plan selected), `SelectPlan` (custom),
`GunaCoupon` (custom), `InitiateCheckout` (browser + CAPI), `Purchase` (CAPI, live).

---

## The constraint that shapes everything

Meta needs roughly **50 optimisation events per ad set per week** to exit the
learning phase. RaikanBersama sells RM29–69 products to a niche audience, so real
`Purchase` volume will start near zero. Therefore:

- **Do not launch Sales campaigns optimising for `Purchase` on day one.** Meta will
  never learn and will spend your budget exploring blindly.
- Start on cheaper, higher-volume events (`ViewContent` / `InitiateCheckout` /
  `Landing Page Views`), collect purchase history, then graduate to `Purchase`.
- Expect the first 30 days to be **demand generation**, not profit.

If average order value lands around RM45, a healthy target is **CPA ≤ RM15**
(ROAS ≈ 3.0). Anything above RM25 CPA burns money at these price points.

## Campaign menu

### Group A — Awareness (cheap reach, builds the retargeting pool)

| # | Campaign | Objective | Audience | Angle | Budget/day |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | **Kad Kahwin Digital — Intro** | Awareness (Reach / Video Views) | MY, 22–38, Malay, engaged + wedding interests | "Jemputan kahwin dalam satu link" — 15s Reel showing open → scroll → RSVP → map | RM10–20 |
| A2 | **Design Showcase Reels** | Awareness / Engagement | Same, broad | Silent Reel carousel of 3–4 catalog designs, music-driven | RM10–15 |
| A3 | **Brand Trust Builder** | Engagement | Page/IG followers + lookalike | Real couple testimonial, WhatsApp screenshots of guests replying | RM8–12 |

Why: awareness here is not vanity — it is the **cheapest way to fill retargeting
audiences** before you have buyers. Target CPM RM15–30.

### Group B — Consideration (traffic into the funnel)

| # | Campaign | Objective | Landing | Angle | Budget/day |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | **Catalog Explorer** | Traffic → Landing Page Views | `/catalog` | "50+ design, pilih ikut tema majlis" | RM10–20 |
| B2 | **Feature Deep-Dive: RSVP** | Traffic | `/` | "Tetamu RSVP sendiri, tak perlu telefon satu-satu" | RM10–15 |
| B3 | **Feature Deep-Dive: Peta & Kalendar** | Traffic | `/` | "Tetamu tak sesat, tak lupa tarikh" | RM8–15 |
| B4 | **Tutorial / How It Works** | Traffic | `/tutorial` | "Siap dalam 5 minit, tanpa designer" | RM8–12 |
| B5 | **Elite Upsell** | Traffic | `/pricing` | "Galeri, hadiah digital, URL unik" | RM10–15 |

Optimise for **Landing Page Views**, not raw clicks — traffic campaigns that
optimise for link clicks attract accidental taps.

### Group C — Conversion (the money campaigns)

| # | Campaign | Objective | Optimisation event | Offer | Budget/day |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | **Pricing — Pro Hero** | Sales | `InitiateCheckout` | Pro RM49 as default choice, "paling popular" | RM20–30 |
| C2 | **Free Draft Start** | Sales / Leads | `InitiateCheckout` or registration | "Buat percuma dulu, bayar bila nak publish" | RM15–25 |
| C3 | **Lite Entry Offer** | Sales | `AddToCart` → `InitiateCheckout` | RM29 lowest-risk entry, upgrade later | RM15–20 |
| C4 | **Discount Flash (7 days)** | Sales | `Purchase` (only if volume allows) | Coupon e.g. `RAIKAN10`, deadline-driven | RM20–30 for 7d |
| C5 | **Bundle / Occasion Promo** | Sales | `InitiateCheckout` | "Tempah awal untuk majlis 2027" | RM15–20 |

Note on C4: a discount campaign only works if the coupon exists and the urgency is
real. Announce it 3 days ahead, run 7 days, then **kill it** — a permanent coupon
destroys your price anchor and trains people to wait.

### Group D — Retargeting (highest ROI, always-on)

| # | Campaign | Audience | Message | Budget/day |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | **Pricing Viewers** | Viewed `/pricing` or `/catalog` 14d, no checkout | Social proof + "RM29 untuk satu jemputan" | RM10–15 |
| D2 | **Checkout Abandoners** | `InitiateCheckout` 7d, no `Purchase` | Objection handling: "Tak pasti? Buat percuma dulu" or small coupon | RM10–15 |
| D3 | **Coupon Bouncers** | `GunaCoupon` 14d, no purchase | "Kod anda masih sah" + expiry urgency | RM5–10 |
| D4 | **Design Browsers** | `AddToCart`/`SelectPlan` 21d, no purchase | "Design pilihan anda masih tersedia" | RM8–12 |
| D5 | **Past Buyers — Referral/Upgrade** | `Purchase` 180d | Upgrade to Elite, or refer a friend (affiliate) | RM5–10 |

Retargeting excludes: purchasers (except D5), and each audience should exclude
the others where messages overlap, so one person doesn't see four ads.

### Group E — B2B / Affiliate (vendor acquisition)

| # | Campaign | Objective | Audience | Angle | Budget/day |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | **Vendor Partner Recruitment** | Leads / Traffic to `/affiliates` | Photographers, videographers, MUA, bridal boutiques, event planners (MY) | "Jual jemputan digital dalam pakej anda, komisen 20–25%" | RM10–15 |
| E2 | **Vendor Retargeting** | Traffic | Visited `/affiliates`, didn't register | "Kira pendapatan sampingan anda" + earnings example | RM5–8 |

This is arguably the **highest-leverage campaign** at your price point: one vendor
resells repeatedly and costs nothing to acquire again. Commission tiers in the
product already climb 20% → 22% → 25%, which is a real ad hook.

### Group F — Seasonal bursts

| # | Window | Campaign | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | Nov–Dec | **Wedding Season Push** | Peak year-end weddings + school holidays |
| F2 | Ramadan → Syawal | **Raya Wedding Prep** | Post-Raya wedding wave is a known MY pattern |
| F3 | Jan–Feb | **Engagement Season** | Valentine engagements → couples start planning |
| F4 | 11.11 / 12.12 | **Flash Sale** | Local e-commerce habit; small coupon, 48h only |
| F5 | 6–12 months before a date | **"Save The Date" Early Bird** | Prevents competitor capture during planning |

### Group G — Scale (only after data)

| # | Campaign | Prerequisite |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | **Advantage+ Sales (broad)** | 50+ purchases in pixel history |
| G2 | **Lookalike 1% of Purchasers** | 100+ purchasers, or 1,000+ `InitiateCheckout` |
| G3 | **Advantage+ Catalog Sales** | A real product feed. "Catalog = website URL" is *not* a feed — this needs a catalog feed build |
| G4 | **Incrementality / geo holdout** | RM1,000+/week spend, to prove ads aren't just harvesting organic |

## Recommended 30-day sequence

| Days | Running | Daily total |
|---|---|---|
| 1–7 | A1, A2, B1, D1 | ~RM50 |
| 8–14 | + C1, B2, D2 | ~RM80 |
| 15–30 | + C3, E1, D3 | ~RM110 |

Do **not** launch everything at once. Adding 2–3 campaigns per week lets you
attribute changes; launching 10 simultaneously means you learn nothing.

## Budget shape

**Starter (RM1,500/month ≈ RM50/day)**
- 55% conversion (C1, C3)
- 25% consideration (B1, B2)
- 20% retargeting (D1, D2)
- Vendor campaign E1 paused until the funnel converts.

**Growth (RM4,000/month ≈ RM130/day)**
- 50% conversion (C1, C3, C4)
- 20% consideration (B1, B2, B5)
- 20% retargeting (D1–D4)
- 10% vendor (E1, E2)

## KPIs to judge each campaign

| Metric | Healthy range | Kill if |
|---|---|---|
| CTR (link) | 1.5–3% | <0.8% after 3 days + RM30 spend |
| CPC | RM0.40–1.50 | >RM3 |
| Cost per Landing Page View | RM1–4 | >RM8 |
| Add-to-cart rate (of LPV) | 5–10% | <2% |
| IC → Purchase rate | 25–40% | <10% |
| CPA | ≤RM15 | >RM25 |
| ROAS | ≥2.5 | <1.5 |

Kill rules matter more than targeting — most beginners lose money by "giving it
more time" on creatives that already failed.

## What NOT to run (yet)

- **Sales optimising for `Purchase`** — not enough volume; learning phase never ends.
- **Lead ads** for a RM29 product — adds friction for an impulse purchase.
- **App install / catalog sales** — no app, no product feed.
- **Lookalikes** before 100 conversion events — garbage seed, garbage audience.
- **Boosted posts** expecting sales — boosts optimise for engagement, not revenue.
- **Permanent discounts** — kills margin and teaches customers to wait.

## Gaps to fix before spending (code, not ads)

1. **`ViewContent` is never called** on `/catalog` or `/pricing` even though the
   helper exists — so Group D audiences (D1, D4) will be thin or empty.
2. **No registration/lead event** — `C2` (free draft start) and `E1` (vendor signup)
   have no conversion to optimise on.
3. **No `Lead` event for affiliates** — E1 must optimise on landing page views instead.
4. **Catalog ads** need a real product feed; do not attempt Advantage+ catalog yet.

Item 1 and 2 are small code changes and directly determine whether retargeting
works at all.
